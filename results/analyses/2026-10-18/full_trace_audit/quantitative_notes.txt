INTERPRETATION
Behavioral impairment is secure. The aggregate confidence decline is not robust to either cue wording or whether the newline is scored. Avoid claim that reasoning generally erases belief in the correct record. Class-only sensitivity retains many individual reversals, so the phenomenon is not entirely a formatting artifact. Show final answer failures and trace-grounded changes; report both original prespecified scores and sensitivity.
Fixed checkpoints sample increasingly selected long trajectories: original only37/112 survive to1024; lookup only25/112 survive to128 and none to256. Cross-sectional checkpoint means are not a single longitudinal curve. Matched deltas or explicitly held fixed cohorts are needed. Preanswer is immediately before an ANSWER marker, usually after substantive conclusions already state the selected class, so endpoint concordance partly reflects visible answer commitment already in the prefix.
Conditional readouts append artificial answer cues to saved prefixes; they are not direct latent-belief measurements. All readouts here are fixed/preanswer; no raw semantic-event interventions or probing/activation data appear in upload.
